# KPI Definitions

## 1. Revenue Growth %
Measures monthly revenue growth performance.

Formula:
(Current Month Revenue - Previous Month Revenue) / Previous Month Revenue

---

## 2. SLA Compliance %
Measures how many service requests were resolved within SLA targets.

Formula:
Resolved Within SLA / Total Requests

---

## 3. Incident Rate
Tracks the number of operational incidents over time.

Formula:
Total Incidents / Reporting Period

---

## 4. Risk Severity Index
Measures the severity level of operational risks.

Formula:
Weighted Risk Score

---

## 5. Operational Efficiency %
Measures operational productivity and performance.

Formula:
Completed Tasks / Total Tasks

---

## 6. Budget Variance
Measures the difference between planned and actual spending.

Formula:
Actual Budget - Planned Budget
