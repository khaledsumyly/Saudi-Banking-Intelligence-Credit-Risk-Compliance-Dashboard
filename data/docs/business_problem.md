# Business Problem

Saudi banks manage large volumes of customer, loan, transaction, and compliance data across multiple departments. Decision-makers need a centralized intelligence dashboard to monitor customer behavior, credit risk, digital adoption, and compliance exceptions.

The goal of this project is to build a Power BI decision-support dashboard that helps banking leaders identify high-risk customers, monitor loan portfolio health, track digital transformation adoption, and detect policy breaches or approval exceptions.

## Project Objective

Build an executive-level banking intelligence solution that combines:

- Customer Intelligence
- Credit Risk Monitoring
- Digital Channel Adoption
- Compliance & Governance Flags
- Executive KPI Tracking

## Target Users

- Executive Management
- Credit Risk Department
- Compliance Team
- Digital Transformation Team
- Customer Analytics Team
